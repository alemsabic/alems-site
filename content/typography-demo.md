## Lists and Organization

### Unordered Lists
- **Primary point**: Vestibulum ante ipsum primis
- **Secondary point**: Faucibus orci luctus et ultrices
  - Nested item one
  - Nested item two
- **Tertiary point**: Posuere cubilia curae

### Ordered Lists
1. **First step**: Mauris blandit aliquet elit
2. **Second step**: Eget dolor vel est condimentum
   1. Sub-step A
   2. Sub-step B
3. **Final step**: Lacinia viverra maecenas
